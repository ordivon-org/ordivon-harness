## 2. Final technology-neutral relation stack

Campaign 3 closes without a universal scalar `Effect Status`.

For an Invocation and an explicit bounded Realization Claim `Q`:

### 2.1 Evidence Projection Validity

Only admissible, correctly bound provenance/authority/currentness evidence may affect Q standing.

### 2.2 Dispatch Standing

Harness separately carries whether dispatch is established, non-dispatch is established, or dispatch remains unresolved.

### 2.3 Realization Claim Contract

`Q` identifies the external realization proposition actually being evaluated. Harness may use a claim grounded by Invocation/capability/external-owner semantics; it must not invent arbitrary physical/domain causal propositions.

If the required claim is external and unavailable, return `EXTERNAL_REALIZATION_CLAIM_REQUIRED`.

### 2.4 Realization Evidence Standing for Q

Harness carries evidence-relative standing only:

- supported by admissible evidence;
- contradicted by admissible evidence;
- evidence conflicted;
- underdetermined.

Underdetermined is not false.

Multi-part/partial realization is represented through a scoped Q set with mixed standings unless an external owner defines a legitimate aggregate claim.

### 2.5 Reconciliation / Settlement Standing

For a bounded operational use, Harness determines whether the relevant Q set is sufficiently settled, requires reconciliation, lacks evidence or requires external decision.

Settlement is use-relative; it does not mean external causal reality is globally/finally closed.

### 2.6 Continuation / Redispatch Obligation

Effect-related standing can block or condition further operation. It does not by itself create a universal retry-safe permission. Repeat/idempotency may require explicitly external capability/domain semantics.

## 3. Runtime / external physical truth boundary

**PASS.**

Runtime/external owners can be authoritative issuers for bounded claims they actually own: dispatch admission, process/execution state, observations, job evidence and other explicitly defined realization claims.

Campaign 3 does not weaken that authority. It limits scope expansion:

`authoritative receipt for Q != authority over every broader physical/world consequence`.

A Runtime receipt may decisively support `Q_runtime_execution`; it need not settle `Q_domain_goal` or arbitrary downstream causal state.

Harness therefore mediates Q/evidence standing without becoming physical-effect truth owner.

`Realization Claim Standing != domain semantic success`.

## 13. Derived laws admitted from Campaign 3

### Realization Standing is Claim-Scope and Evidence Relative

Harness evaluates explicit bounded realization claims from admissible evidence; it does not own one global world Effect variable.

### Dispatch Standing != Realization Claim Standing

Boundary dispatch and external realization evidence are distinct relations.

### No Receipt != No Effect != Retry Permission

Missing terminal evidence after dispatch establishes neither non-realization nor universal permission to repeat.

### Local Terminality != External Causal Finality != Semantic Success

Local/issuer-owned terminal standing can coexist with unresolved broader consequences and domain failure/unknown.

### Partial Realization is Scoped Mixed Claim Standing

Without an owner-defined aggregate, partiality is mixed evidence standing over explicit scoped claims rather than one universal completion scalar.

### Invocation Cardinality != External Realization Cardinality

Harness logical Invocation identity/fencing does not universally determine the number of external realization events.

### Compensation != Prior Effect Erasure

A compensating operation is a later operational/claim edge and does not erase prior supported realization provenance.

### Reconciliation Settlement is Use-Relative and History-Preserving

Reconciliation may settle a bounded downstream use while preserving prior nonterminal/uncertain/conflicted evidence in lineage.

These are project-level derived laws, not Foundations.
