# Operational Accountability Graph
## Claim Support, Dependency, Contestability & Bounded Reproduction

**Control task:** `task:harness-campaign4-operational-accountability-20260819`  
**Programme:** E — Accountability & Assurance  
**Standing effect:** programme-level investigation only. HaF0–HaF61 remain frozen; HaF62 remains UNKNOWN / NOT SELECTED / NOT ADMITTED.

## 1. Research question

For an explicit bounded operational claim `C`, what support/dependency structure may Harness responsibly preserve so that the claim is inspectable, reproducible and contestable, while retaining provenance, currentness, counterevidence and unknowns — without turning evidence into truth, graph reachability into proof, or attribution into normative blame?

Campaign 4 does **not** ask Harness to determine arbitrary external/domain truth. It asks how Harness should represent the operational basis on which a bounded claim is currently carried.

## 2. Terminology guard

`Operational Accountability Graph` is a project label, not a commitment to one graph database, node schema or universal ontology.

Round 1 may revise the core term to **Operational Claim Support Structure** or another technology-neutral relation if `graph` invites accidental reachability/transitivity semantics.

## 3. Ownership firewall

Harness may own:

- bounded operational claim identity/scope;
- binding of Run/Context/Invocation/Result/Realization-Claim objects to that claim;
- provenance/currentness of evidence projections;
- typed evidence bearing on the claim;
- explicit dependency/common-cause relations when known;
- unresolved unknown and counterevidence preservation;
- bounded operational attribution and reproduction contracts;
- contest/revision lineage of the accountability state.

Harness does not own:

- arbitrary World/domain truth;
- normative blame, liability, desert or legitimacy;
- human phenomenological explanation quality;
- Runtime physical truth beyond externally grounded claims;
- Network substrate truth.

## 4. Prebound relation stack

For claim `C` and bounded **Accountability Use Contract** `A`:

### Layer 0 — Claim Contract

`C` must identify what is actually being claimed, its subject/scope, relevant owner/issuer role, and the operational use for which accountability is requested.

If the claim cannot be scoped without external/domain semantics, Harness must require an external claim contract rather than inventing one.

### Layer 1 — Evidence Admission Validity

Evidence `E` must be admissible under provenance, authority, identity binding, currentness/version and representation rules.

`valid evidence != support for C`.

### Layer 2 — Typed Bearing on C

An admitted item can have different relation to C:

- `SUPPORTS_C`
- `CONTRADICTS_C`
- `QUALIFIES_C`
- `CONTEXT_FOR_C`
- `NO_DIRECT_BEARING_ON_C`
- `BEARING_UNDERDETERMINED`

The exact vocabulary may be revised, but one universal `evidence=true` relation is prohibited.

### Layer 3 — Dependency / Independence Standing

Accountability must preserve known evidence dependencies such as:

- copied/derived from;
- common upstream source;
- independently originated under the bounded criterion;
- dependency unknown.

Artifact multiplicity does not imply independent corroboration.

### Layer 4 — Currentness / Contest / Unknown Standing

The structure must preserve:

- stale/superseded vs current standing;
- admissible counterevidence/rebuttal;
- unresolved unknowns required by `A`;
- whether the current support set is contested or incomplete.

### Layer 5 — Attribution Binding

Operational results/evidence must bind to the correct Run/Context/Invocation/Realization Claim/contract lineage. Attribution identifies semantic production/binding; it does not by itself assign normative blame.

### Layer 6 — Bounded Reproduction Standing

A reproduction contract asks whether another investigator/Run can reconstruct the relevant support basis/conditions strongly enough for `A`. It does not promise identical model output or identical external-world outcome.

### Layer 7 — Accountability Standing

For the bounded use `A`, Harness may classify whether C has an adequate inspectable support structure, remains contested/underdetermined, or requires external/domain decision.

Campaign 4 deliberately does not prebind one universal probability/confidence score.

## 5. Proposition P4 — Claim-Relative Typed Accountability

A technology-neutral Harness accountability criterion can remain owner-preserving if accountability is represented as a claim-relative typed support/dependency structure rather than a flat trace/evidence list or universal truth graph.

### P4-A — Claim identity/scope precedes evidence sufficiency

An artifact cannot support an unspecified or broader claim merely because it is authentic.

### P4-B — Evidence validity != support bearing

Valid provenance establishes what an artifact is/from whom/when, not whether it supports C.

### P4-C — Support is typed and non-transitive by default

`E supports C1` plus any arbitrary `C1 -> C2` relation does not imply `E supports C2`. Support composition requires explicit typed semantics.

### P4-D — Artifact count != corroboration count

Dependent/copied/common-cause artifacts do not gain independent evidential standing through multiplicity.

### P4-E — Same content != same evidential role

Two byte-identical items may differ in authority, independence, currentness or claim bearing; two differently encoded items may independently support the same bounded claim.

### P4-F — Unknown/counterevidence omission can invalidate bounded accountability

A support presentation that hides `A`-required unresolved unknowns or admissible counterevidence is not an adequate accountability projection even if all positive evidence is authentic.

### P4-G — Currentness revision preserves history

Superseding or rebutting evidence can change current support standing without deleting historical support/counterevidence lineage.

### P4-H — CompletionProposal is evidence/proposal, not completion authority

A valid CompletionProposal may support a bounded claim that the Harness Run proposes completion; it does not establish caller/domain/Host completion.

### P4-I — Result attribution is exact-binding relative

A Result/receipt/trace from another Run/contract/Context/Invocation cannot be silently reused to support C merely because the names/content look similar.

### P4-J — Realization evidence != semantic-success evidence

Campaign 3 realization claim support cannot be promoted to domain semantic success without a domain-owned support edge/claim.

### P4-K — Bounded reproduction != identical outcome replay

Accountability reproduction concerns reconstructing the support basis/conditions required by A; external nondeterminism may still change outcomes.

### P4-L — Contestability is revision-capable, not history-destructive

New rebuttal/counterevidence must be able to weaken/change current standing while preserving prior support lineage.

### P4-M — Operational attribution != normative blame

Harness may identify which Run/Invocation/Agent-facing operation produced or adopted a claim/result; responsibility/blame/liability requires Normative/Human/domain semantics.

## 6. Rival models

### R1 — Trace Equals Accountability

A complete event/log trace is treated as sufficient accountability regardless of claim scope/support semantics.

### R2 — Provenance Equals Support

Authentic provenance is treated as proof that the artifact supports the target claim.

### R3 — Flat Evidence List

A list of evidence refs is treated as sufficient without typed bearing/dependency/currentness/counterevidence structure.

### R4 — Artifact Count Equals Corroboration

More artifacts are treated as stronger independent support regardless of dependency/common cause.

### R5 — Receipt Equals Proof

A valid Run/Tool/Provider receipt is treated as proof of any broader claim associated with the operation.

### R6 — Graph Reachability Equals Support

Any directed path from E to C is treated as evidential support; edge types/scope do not matter.

### R7 — Newest Evidence Wins

The newest-looking artifact automatically supersedes prior evidence without explicit authority/currentness relation.

### R8 — CompletionProposal Equals Completion

Harness candidate completion is treated as caller/domain/Host completion.

### R9 — Reproduction Equals Identical Outcome

A reproducible support basis is required to reproduce identical model/world output.

### R10 — No Counterevidence Recorded Means Uncontested Truth

Absence of recorded contradiction is treated as proof that C is uncontested/true.

### R11 — Attribution Equals Blame

Operational producer/adopter attribution is treated as normative responsibility/liability.

### R12 — Support Is Universally Transitive

Support propagates over arbitrary claim/relation chains without explicit composition semantics.

## 7. Prebound falsifier classes

P4 must be revised/rejected if:

### F1 — External truth annexation

Adequate accountability cannot be stated without Harness deciding arbitrary domain truth.

### F2 — Dependency blindness

The criterion cannot distinguish copied/common-cause evidence from bounded independent corroboration.

### F3 — Counterevidence blindness

Authentic positive evidence keeps C “adequately supported” after relevant admissible contradiction/rebuttal that A requires to be visible.

### F4 — Unknown suppression

A required unresolved unknown can be omitted without changing accountability standing.

### F5 — Binding blindness

Evidence from the wrong Run/Context/Invocation/contract can support C merely by matching names/content.

### F6 — Support reachability collapse

Untyped graph path/reachability is enough to generate support.

### F7 — Completion authority collapse

CompletionProposal/Run terminality becomes caller/domain/Host completion.

### F8 — Reproduction overclaim

The criterion requires identical external/model outcomes rather than bounded support-basis reconstruction.

### F9 — Normative blame annexation

Operational attribution necessarily implies liability/blame/legitimacy.

### F10 — History rewrite

Rebuttal/currentness revision cannot preserve prior support lineage.

### F11 — Circular support acceptance

A claim can become supported only because C and its derived claims cyclically support each other with no admissible external/primitive support edge.

### F12 — Hidden implementation dependence

The criterion requires current receipt classes, SQLite layout or one graph storage technology.

## 9. Prebound expected tendencies

Predictions, not results:

- T1 -> `SUPPORT_UNDERDETERMINED/INADEQUATE_FOR_A`, not false-by-default.
- T2 -> valid evidence with `NO_DIRECT_BEARING_ON_C`.
- T3 -> invalid evidence projection.
- T4 -> artifact multiplicity but one dependency family; no independent corroboration gain.
- T5 -> potentially independent corroboration despite same content if independence criteria are externally/operationally satisfied.
- T6/T7 -> currentness requires explicit relation, not timestamp recency alone.
- T8/T9 -> accountability projection must preserve required unknown/counterevidence.
- T10 -> supports proposal claim only.
- T11/T12/T20 -> support fails when exact lineage/binding obligations fail.
- T13/T14 -> external/domain support edge required rather than semantic promotion.
- T15 -> reproduction can succeed even if nondeterministic world/model outcome differs, provided A only requires support-basis reconstruction.
- T16 -> current standing may revise without history deletion.
- T17/T18 -> reachability/cycles do not create support without valid typed composition/external support.
- T19 -> some metadata-level accountability claims may remain inspectable while content-dependent claims remain unreproducible/underdetermined.

## 10. Evaluation method

### Round 1 — Conceptual destructive reconstruction

Apply P4 and R1–R12 to T1–T20. Explicitly test whether `Accountability Graph` should be demoted to a presentation term in favor of **Operational Claim Support Structure**.

### Round 2 — Existing engineering dogfood only

Use current receipt/proposal/prior-evidence/strategy-evidence/unknown-preservation/telemetry fixtures. No new graph implementation or tests are permitted solely to support P4.

### Round 3 — Owner-boundary + Campaign 1–3 compatibility audit

Verify domain truth, Normative blame, Human explanation, Runtime/Network truth boundaries. Test composition with Run identity, Context sufficiency and Realization Claim standing.

### Round 4 — Closeout

Classify:

- `CRITERION_SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE`
- `CRITERION_REVISED`
- `CRITERION_FALSIFIED`
- `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`

Foundation pressure is separate.

## 11. Stop conditions

Stop rather than expand scope if:

- deciding whether C is actually true becomes the primary problem;
- blame/liability/legitimacy becomes primary;
- human explanation quality becomes primary;
- a universal graph database/schema is required;
- arbitrary support composition requires domain semantics unavailable to Harness;
- no existing engineering fixture is information-positive for a conceptual case.
