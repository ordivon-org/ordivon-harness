## 3. Candidate C1 — Campaign-4 rebuttal / counterevidence revision

**READY NOW. SELECTED.**

Campaign 4 explicitly closed with general rebuttal/counterevidence revision lacking direct engineering proof. Claim Standing v1 now provides the exact minimal product semantics needed to attack the narrow claim:

- one Q initially locally `SUPPORTED`;
- exact counterevidence becomes visible but is not admitted;
- standing remains unchanged;
- counterevidence is explicitly admitted in a later projection generation;
- standing becomes `CONFLICTED` while Q and old support/history remain intact;
- an independent local projection with counterevidence-only basis becomes `CONTRADICTED`;
- required unknown remains first-class and can dominate positive evidence into `UNDERDETERMINED`;
- no global mutable claim status or evidence erasure is permitted.

This directly pressures Campaign-4 laws:

- counterevidence / required unknowns are first-class accountability content;
- provenance/admission/bearing/standing are distinct;
- contest does not rewrite history;
- positive support does not immunize a claim from later counterevidence.

The branch can use the current public API only. No production patch is necessary.
