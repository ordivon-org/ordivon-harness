# Conceptual Destructive Reconstruction

**Prebinding authority:** Campaign 4 Charter v1 at commit `439ef56` before this analysis.  
**Round type:** conceptual/destructive. Current receipt/trace/evidence-ref objects are not ontology authority.

## 1. Round 1 classification

`CRITERION_REVISED`.

P4 survives its central claim: bounded Harness accountability can be owner-preserving only when operational claims are carried with typed support/dependency/currentness/counterevidence/unknown/attribution/reproduction structure rather than a flat evidence list or truth graph.

Three substantive revisions are required.

### Revision 1 — `Accountability Graph` is presentation, not semantic primitive

Graph topology alone has no evidential meaning. Reachability, path count, centrality or cycles cannot generate support standing without typed relations and explicit composition rules.

The technology-neutral core is renamed **Operational Claim Support Structure (OCSS)**. A graph is one possible projection of that structure.

### Revision 2 — independence must never be inferred from “no known dependency”

Campaign 4 must distinguish:

- `DEPENDENCY_ESTABLISHED`;
- `INDEPENDENCE_ESTABLISHED_FOR_CRITERION K`;
- `DEPENDENCY_STANDING_UNRESOLVED`.

Unknown dependency is not independent corroboration.

Independence is itself criterion-relative: two observations may be independently generated by one criterion yet share a deeper common world source. Harness must preserve the declared criterion rather than promote independence globally.

### Revision 3 — operational attribution is typed role binding

One generic `attributed_to` edge is too coarse. At minimum, accountability must distinguish roles such as:

- `PRODUCED_BY`;
- `OBSERVED_BY`;
- `ASSERTED_BY`;
- `ADOPTED_BY`;
- `MEDIATED_BY`;
- `REALIZED_BY_EXTERNAL_OWNER` where externally grounded.

These are operational bindings, not blame or moral responsibility.

Therefore P4 is revised to P4'.

## 2. Revised technology-neutral core — OCSS

For an explicit claim `C` and bounded **Accountability Use Contract** `A`, an Operational Claim Support Structure contains only relations necessary to explain the current operational support standing of C.

### 2.1 Claim Contract

C identifies:

- proposition/claim identity;
- subject and scope;
- owner/issuer role where relevant;
- temporal/version scope;
- bounded accountability use A.

If the proposition itself is external/domain-owned and cannot be safely scoped by Harness, return `EXTERNAL_CLAIM_CONTRACT_REQUIRED`.

### 2.2 Evidence Admission

Each evidence item E has admission standing based on provenance, authority, identity binding, representation validity and currentness lineage.

Authentic evidence can still be irrelevant, stale, superseded or non-supporting.

### 2.3 Typed Bearing

For C, each admitted E has an explicit relation such as:

- `SUPPORTS`;
- `CONTRADICTS`;
- `QUALIFIES`;
- `CONTEXT_ONLY`;
- `NO_DIRECT_BEARING`;
- `BEARING_UNDERDETERMINED`.

No generic “evidence attached” edge carries support semantics.

### 2.4 Dependency / Independence

Evidence relationships preserve known derivation/common-cause standing. Multiplicity changes support only if A requires and the relevant independence criterion is actually established.

### 2.5 Currentness / Contest / Unknowns

OCSS preserves:

- current vs stale/superseded standing;
- admissible rebuttal/counterevidence;
- unresolved unknowns required by A;
- support gaps;
- dependency uncertainty where material.

### 2.6 Typed Attribution

Production, observation, assertion, adoption, mediation and external realization roles are separate. These bindings support inspectability but do not imply normative responsibility.

### 2.7 Bounded Reproduction

Reproduction standing concerns whether A-required support basis/conditions can be reconstructed/re-inspected. It may fail for content-dependent claims even when metadata-level claims remain inspectable.

### 2.8 Accountability Adequacy

`Adequate(C,A)` means the OCSS satisfies the explicit support/visibility/reproduction obligations declared by A without hiding required counterevidence/unknowns or using invalid support composition.

`Adequate(C,A)` does not mean C is true.

## 3. Proposition P4'

### P4'-A — Accountability adequacy is claim- and use-contract-relative

There is no global “accountable=true” property independent of C and A.

### P4'-B — Valid provenance != evidential bearing

Provenance authenticates origin/binding; bearing asks what the item does for C.

### P4'-C — Bearing is explicit and non-transitive by default

Support propagates only through an admitted typed composition rule whose scope/owner semantics justify it.

### P4'-D — Evidence multiplicity != independent corroboration

Artifact count is descriptive, not corroboration cardinality.

### P4'-E — Unknown dependency != independence

Independence must be positively established for a bounded criterion when A relies on it.

### P4'-F — Same bytes != same evidential role

Authority/currentness/dependency/claim scope can differ even when content is identical.

### P4'-G — Counterevidence and required unknowns are accountability content

Accountability can become inadequate through omission even when every included positive artifact is authentic.

### P4'-H — Currentness revision preserves evidential history

A rebuttal or successor authority may change current support standing while historical support remains inspectable.

### P4'-I — CompletionProposal supports only its bounded proposal claim

It cannot be promoted into Host/caller/domain completion authority.

### P4'-J — Exact attribution/binding matters

Evidence from another Run/Context/Invocation/contract does not silently transfer support through naming similarity or byte similarity.

### P4'-K — Realization support does not compose automatically into semantic-success support

Campaign 3 Q standing can support only compatible claims unless an owner-authorized composition edge exists.

### P4'-L — Reproduction is obligation-relative reconstruction, not identical outcome

An OCSS can be reproducible for A even when external/model outputs are nondeterministic.

### P4'-M — Operational attribution is role-typed and normatively incomplete

`PRODUCED_BY`, `ASSERTED_BY`, `ADOPTED_BY`, etc. do not imply blame/liability/desert.

### P4'-N — Circular support does not self-ground

A support cycle without an admissible grounding edge cannot bootstrap adequate support.

### P4'-O — Contestability requires revision without erasure

Admissible rebuttal can weaken or change current support while preserving the previous accountability state.

## 4. Fixture evaluation

### T1 — C asserted with no support path

Result: C is not thereby false. OCSS records an unsupported/underdetermined support state; `Adequate(C,A)` fails when A requires support.

This rejects truth-by-default and absence-of-proof confusion.

### T2 — authentic artifact irrelevant to C

Result: evidence admission can be valid while bearing is `NO_DIRECT_BEARING`.

R2 Provenance Equals Support is falsified.

### T3 — forged relevant-looking evidence

Result: invalid at Evidence Admission. Apparent relevance cannot repair forged provenance/binding.

### T4 — three copied artifacts from one source

Result: artifact count = 3; independent corroboration count is not 3. Dependency/common-cause standing collapses the evidential independence claim while retaining three historical artifacts.

R4 is falsified.

### T5 — two independently originated same-content sources

Result: same bytes/content do not prevent independent corroboration if independence is positively established for the relevant A criterion.

Important revision: “different bytes” is neither necessary nor sufficient for independence.

### T6 — current positive source plus stale historical source

Result: both remain inspectable; only the explicitly current standing may satisfy a current-state A. Historical source remains evidence of prior support state, not duplicate current support.

### T7 — newer-looking source with no supersession authority conflicts with current source

Result: timestamp/recency alone cannot supersede. Preserve conflict/currentness relation until authority is established.

R7 Newest Evidence Wins is falsified.

### T8 — positive evidence but required unresolved unknown omitted

Result: inadequate accountability projection for A. The included support may remain valid, but the presentation is misleading because it suppresses a required unknown.

F4 is directly confirmed conceptually.

### T9 — positive evidence plus admissible counterevidence

Result: current support becomes contested/qualified according to bearing and authority; positive support is not deleted. Counterevidence does not necessarily negate C — it changes OCSS standing according to claim-compatible semantics.

### T10 — CompletionProposal used to claim Host/domain completion

Result: invalid support composition. CompletionProposal can support `C_proposal = Harness Run proposed completion`, not `C_host_completed` or arbitrary domain success.

R8 falsified.

### T11 — Run Receipt reused across changed mandate/contract

Result: binding fails if A requires exact mandate/contract lineage. Similar run/profile name does not transfer support.

### T12 — Result attributed to wrong Invocation/Run/Context

Result: accountability invalid for exact-binding use even if bytes are authentic. Operational attribution requires correct semantic lineage.

### T13 — realization Q supported; domain success lacks domain edge

Result: Q support remains valid, but semantic-success C is unsupported/externally unresolved. Campaign-3 owner firewall survives.

### T14 — cross-owner claim with no explicit authority edge

Result: `EXTERNAL_CLAIM_CONTRACT_REQUIRED` or inadequate support for A. Harness cannot manufacture the missing external owner relation.

### T15 — support basis reproduced, external result differs

Result: reproduction can be adequate when A requires exact support inputs/provenance/conditions rather than identical output. Outcome equality is a separate claim.

R9 Reproduction Equals Identical Outcome is falsified.

### T16 — rebuttal weakens current standing while prior support remains

Result: OCSS revision appends a new accountability state/currentness relation. Historical support is retained.

R10/F10 history-destructive rivals fail.

### T17 — C1 supported; C1 merely associated with C2

Result: no automatic support propagation. A typed composition rule is required. `associated_with` is not `supports`.

R6/R12 are falsified.

### T18 — C1 and C2 mutually support with no grounding

Result: cycle does not self-ground. Both remain underdetermined unless at least one admissible external/primitive support edge plus valid composition exists.

### T19 — terminal receipt digest survives but private content unavailable

Result: metadata-level claims such as “a bound receipt with digest D was admitted” may remain inspectable; content-semantic claims may be unreproducible/underdetermined. Accountability is claim-specific, not all-or-nothing.

### T20 — same evidence bytes in successor Run under changed Context

Result: same bytes do not transfer support automatically. If A depends on old Context/Run standing, the successor must explicitly adopt/rebind the evidence under Campaign-1/2 relations.

## 6. Derived-law candidates

### L-C4-1 — Accountability Graph is a Projection; Typed Claim Support is the Semantics

Graph topology/storage is non-authoritative without typed claim-relative relations.

### L-C4-2 — Provenance Validity != Evidential Bearing != Accountability Adequacy

Authentic origin, support relation and adequacy for a bounded use are distinct.

### L-C4-3 — Support is Non-Transitive by Default

Support composition requires an explicit typed rule; reachability is insufficient.

### L-C4-4 — Unknown Dependency != Independence

Independent corroboration must be established under a bounded criterion rather than inferred from missing dependency knowledge.

### L-C4-5 — Counterevidence / Required Unknowns are First-Class Accountability Content

Omitting relevant contradiction or unknowns can make a support presentation inadequate even when positive evidence is authentic.

### L-C4-6 — Operational Attribution is Typed Role Binding, not Normative Blame

Production/assertion/adoption/observation/mediation roles must not collapse into liability/responsibility.

### L-C4-7 — Bounded Reproduction != Outcome Identity

Reproducing support basis/conditions does not guarantee identical model/world result.

### L-C4-8 — Support Cycles Do Not Self-Ground

Circular claim relations require independent admissible grounding and valid composition.

These remain provisional until closeout.

## 7. Campaign 1–3 compatibility

### Campaign 1 — Identity

Evidence support is bound to Run/Invocation lineage; lineage ancestry does not automatically transfer support. A successor Run may adopt evidence but must satisfy explicit binding rules.

### Campaign 2 — Context

Accountability presentations are Context projections. Omitting A-required unknown/counterevidence/currentness can make a Context insufficient. Context equivalence does not mean accountability equivalence unless A obligations are preserved.

### Campaign 3 — Realization Claims

Realization Q support becomes one typed claim family inside OCSS. It cannot silently support broader semantic-success claims.

No prior campaign is reopened.
