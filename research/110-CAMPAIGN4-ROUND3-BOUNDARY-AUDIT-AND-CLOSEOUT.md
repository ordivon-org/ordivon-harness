## 2. Final technology-neutral core — OCSS

`Operational Accountability Graph` remains a project/presentation label. The semantic core is **Operational Claim Support Structure (OCSS)**.

For explicit claim `C` and bounded Accountability Use Contract `A`, OCSS preserves:

1. **Claim Contract** — proposition identity, subject/scope, owner/issuer role, temporal/version scope and bounded use;
2. **Evidence Admission** — provenance/authority/identity/currentness-valid evidence projection;
3. **Typed Bearing** — supports / contradicts / qualifies / context-only / no-direct-bearing / underdetermined;
4. **Dependency / Independence Standing** — dependency established, independence established for a bounded criterion, or dependency unresolved;
5. **Currentness / Contest / Unknown Standing** — stale/superseded/current, rebuttal/counterevidence, unresolved unknowns and support gaps;
6. **Typed Operational Attribution** — produced/observed/asserted/adopted/mediated/externally-realized roles as applicable;
7. **Bounded Reproduction Standing** — ability to reconstruct/re-inspect A-required support basis/conditions;
8. **Accountability Adequacy** — whether the structure satisfies A's explicit support/visibility/reproduction obligations.

`Adequate(C,A)` does not assert C is true.

## 3. World / domain truth boundary

**PASS.**

Campaign 4 evaluates the **support structure for a claim**, not arbitrary truth of the claim.

A domain-owned claim may have a fully inspectable OCSS while remaining externally unresolved, contested or false according to the domain owner.

Therefore:

`Accountability Adequacy != Claim Truth`  
`Evidence Bearing != Domain Correctness`.

When claim scope/support composition requires domain semantics unavailable to Harness, return `EXTERNAL_CLAIM_CONTRACT_REQUIRED` rather than inventing a support edge.

## 4. Normative boundary

**PASS.**

Operational attribution can state roles such as `PRODUCED_BY`, `ASSERTED_BY`, `ADOPTED_BY` or `MEDIATED_BY`.

It does not answer:

- who is morally blameworthy;
- who is legally liable;
- whether delegation was legitimate;
- what remedy is deserved.

Those require Normative/domain semantics.

`Operational Attribution != Normative Responsibility`.

## 5. Human boundary

## 15. Derived laws admitted from Campaign 4

### Accountability Graph is a Projection; Typed Claim Support is the Semantics

Graph topology/storage/reachability is non-authoritative without claim-relative typed relations.

### Provenance Validity != Evidential Bearing != Accountability Adequacy

Authentic origin, relevance/support relation and adequacy for bounded use are distinct.

### Support is Non-Transitive by Default

Support composition requires an explicit typed rule with compatible scope/owner semantics; arbitrary reachability is insufficient.

### Unknown Dependency != Independence

Absence of a known dependency does not establish independent corroboration. Independence must be positively established for the bounded criterion that relies on it.

### Counterevidence / Required Unknowns are First-Class Accountability Content

Omitting required contradiction, qualification or unknowns can make an accountability projection inadequate even when positive evidence is authentic.

### Operational Attribution is Typed Role Binding, not Normative Blame

Production/observation/assertion/adoption/mediation roles are distinct operational relations and do not imply liability or moral responsibility.

### Bounded Reproduction != Outcome Identity

Reconstructing/re-inspecting the required support basis/conditions does not guarantee identical model or external-world outcome.

### Support Cycles Do Not Self-Ground

Circular claim/support relations cannot bootstrap adequacy without admissible grounding and valid typed composition.

These are project-level derived laws, not Foundations.
