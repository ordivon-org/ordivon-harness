## 3. Candidate comparison

### C1 — Operational Accountability Graph

**Programme home:** E — Accountability & Assurance.

**Information gain:** very high. It is now the first frontier that directly composes Identity + Context + Realization results.

**Destructive-testability:** very high through missing support edges, forged/stale evidence, common-cause duplicates, mismatched Run/Context/Invocation/result binding, unresolved unknown suppression, CompletionProposal overclaim, and result-attribution errors.

**Architecture-falsification power:** very high. It can invalidate “log = explanation”, “receipt = proof”, “more artifacts = more evidence”, “completion proposal = completion”, and flat provenance structures.

Decision: strongest current Campaign-4 candidate.

### C2 — Boundary Reconfiguration Equivalence

**Information gain:** high.

**Maturity:** now independently researchable because Campaigns 1–3 provide identity, Context and realization-preservation relations.

**Engineering evidence:** meaningful existing morphing/successor-attempt surfaces exist.

**Remaining dependency:** a boundary-equivalence criterion would consume accountability obligations as well as identity/context/effect standing when proving that a reconfigured cut preserves inspectability/authority/evidence. Starting now is viable, but it would be cleaner after bounded accountability obligations are explicit.

Decision: defer one campaign; not rejected.

### C4 — Cross-implementation invariance

**Importance:** high as falsification discipline.

**Classification:** primarily evidence acquisition unless a concrete owner fixture exposes a semantic contradiction. It does not currently define a new Harness-wide theoretical bottleneck.

Decision: maintain as targeted evidence programme/reopen trigger, not Campaign 4.

## 4. Tournament result

**SELECTED CAMPAIGN 4:**

# Operational Accountability Graph
## Claim Support, Dependency, Contestability & Bounded Reproduction

Programme home: **E — Accountability & Assurance**.

### Bounded purpose

Derive and destructively test a technology-neutral Harness criterion for representing **operational claim-support structure** across Run, Context, Invocation, realization evidence, Result and unresolved unknowns, such that claims remain inspectable/reproducible/contestable without annexing external truth or normative blame.

### Explicit non-goals

Campaign 4 will not:

- build a universal truth/knowledge graph;
- decide domain correctness or external truth;
- define normative blame, liability or legitimacy;
- infer independent corroboration from artifact count;
- require one universal graph database/schema;
- solve human explanation quality;
- solve Boundary Reconfiguration or Multi-Agent responsibility;
- create HaF62.

## 5. Required destructive dimensions for Campaign 4

The Charter must prebind at least:

- claim with no support path;
- evidence ref whose provenance/binding is forged;
- two artifacts sharing one dependency mistaken for corroboration;
- independent evidence with same content;
- stale/superseded evidence retained in history;
- claim support that omits a relevant unresolved unknown;
- CompletionProposal treated as caller/domain completion;
- Run Receipt reused across changed contract/Context/mandate;
- Result attributed to wrong Invocation/Run/Context;
- realization claim support confused with semantic-success support;
- support path that crosses owner boundary without an explicit external claim/authority edge;
- reproduction that preserves exact operational support but not identical world outcome;
- contest/rebuttal evidence that weakens current support without deleting history;
- common-cause dependency hidden by multiple artifacts.
