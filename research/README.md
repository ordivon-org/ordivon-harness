## Campaign 3 selection result

The post-Campaign-2 tournament found that both completed campaigns already consume typed external-effect standing while deliberately avoiding a general Harness theory for delayed, partial, duplicate, unknown and compensating realization.

The selected Campaign 3 therefore studies **Operational Effect Standing & Reconciliation**, not universal physical Effect ontology.

Operational Accountability is now the strongest likely downstream consumer, but is deferred one campaign so that an Accountability Graph does not silently collapse attempted/dispatched/effected/observed/result/semantic-success relations.

Boundary Reconfiguration is now mature enough for a later independent campaign. Multi-Agent/Federated identity remains deferred until Effect and Boundary relations are sharper. Cross-implementation invariance remains a mandatory falsification dimension rather than a standalone campaign.
