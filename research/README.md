## Active Campaign 3

Campaign 3 does not assume one universal `effect_status`. The prebound relation stack separates:

1. **Effect Evidence Projection Standing**;
2. **Dispatch Standing**;
3. **Realization-Claim Standing**;
4. **Reconciliation Standing**;
5. **Continuation / Redispatch Obligation**.

The central firewall is that Harness may carry evidence-relative operational claims and obligations but does not become the owner of physical causality or domain semantic success.

The Charter prebinds destructive cases for no-dispatch, response-loss/unknown realization, cancellation races, partial and delayed realization, duplicate/retry hazards, compensation, conflicting evidence, interacting Invocations and semantic-failure-after-physical-effect cases.

Operational Accountability remains downstream rather than absorbed into Campaign 3.
