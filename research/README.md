## Campaign 2 — current result

Round 1 revised the theory from a broad “safe Context substitution” framing to a narrower technology-neutral relation stack:

1. **Context Projection Validity**;
2. **Effective Decision Context** — durable basis plus admitted transient exposure at the frontier;
3. **Decision Sufficiency** relative to a Context Use Contract;
4. **Obligation-Preserving Substitution** (directional);
5. **Operational Context Equivalence** as mutual substitution under the same use contract/horizon.

Current provisional laws:

- **Context Validity != Currentness != Sufficiency**;
- **Durable Context Basis != Effective Decision Context**;
- **Context Sufficiency is Use-Contract and Horizon Relative**;
- **Context Superset != Context Dominance**.

The relation does not claim identical Agent/model behavior or external/domain truth equivalence. Missing domain relevance obligations yield `EXTERNAL_DECISION_CONTRACT_REQUIRED` rather than a guessed relevance verdict.

Current Round 1 standing: `CRITERION_REVISED`, `NO_FOUNDATION_PRESSURE`.
