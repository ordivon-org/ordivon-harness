## 2. Post-Campaign-2 dependency delta

Campaigns 1 and 2 have removed two major blockers:

- Run identity/recovery now has a bounded criterion;
- Context validity/sufficiency/equivalence now has a bounded use-contract/horizon-relative criterion.

Both campaigns nevertheless deliberately consume **typed external-effect standing** without providing a general theory of that standing.

Campaign 1 uses distinctions such as:

- not dispatched;
- possibly effected / unresolved;
- reconciled / externally evidenced;
- settled Invocation without safe redispatch.

Campaign 2 admits Tool observations/evidence into effective Context while preserving the firewall that physical effect truth belongs to Runtime/external owners.

Therefore the remaining gap is no longer “what is physical effect truth?” That question is outside Harness ownership. The Harness-native gap is:

> **Given externally grounded evidence, what operational standing may Harness assign to an Invocation/effect claim, what remains unresolved, and what reconciliation/redispatch/attribution obligations follow without collapsing dispatch, effect, observation, result and semantic success?**

This is better named **Operational Effect Standing & Reconciliation** than a universal “Effect ontology”.

## 3. Candidate comparison

### C1 — Operational Effect Standing & Reconciliation

**Programme home:** C — Agent Action & Effect Semantics.

**Information gain:** very high. It is now the major unresolved relation directly consumed by Campaign 1 recovery and by Context/evidence mediation in Campaign 2.

**Destructive-testability:** very high through response loss, unknown realization, nonterminal/terminal reconciliation, cancel races, duplicate hazards, delayed/partial realization, compensation, contradictory observations and semantic-failure-after-physical-effect cases.

**Architecture-falsification power:** very high. A surviving criterion can revise lifecycle separation, Invocation frontier accounting, reconciliation rules, result attribution and retry/continuation assumptions.

**Engineering evidence:** high but incomplete. Current Harness has Tool intent/dispatch fences, terminal/nonterminal receipts, reconciliation chains, response-loss fencing, fresh-process recovery and metadata-only evidence. Current implementation does **not** establish a full theory for partial/delayed/irreversible/compensating/interfering effects; those remain conceptual/future evidence pressure.

**Owner-boundary risk:** high but controllable if the campaign studies Harness-owned **effect-claim/standing mediation** and consumes Runtime/external evidence instead of deciding physical causality.

**Dependency standing:** prerequisite/conditioning relation for a rigorous Accountability Graph and useful for later Multi-Agent/Boundary work.

### C2 — Operational Accountability Graph

**Information gain:** now high. Campaign 1/2 finally provide sharper Run, Invocation and Context relations.

**Destructive-testability:** high through missing edges, false provenance, copied evidence, stale Context, misattributed result and CompletionProposal/caller-domain boundary errors.

**Engineering evidence:** very high through Run Receipt, CompletionProposal, exact prior-attempt evidence, immutable strategy evidence, unresolved unknowns and telemetry projection.

Without a sharper effect-standing relation, the graph risks hard-coding an unresolved semantic collapse.

## 5. Tournament decision

**SELECTED CAMPAIGN 3:**

# Operational Effect Standing & Reconciliation
## Unknown, Partial, Duplicate, Delayed & Compensating External Realization

Programme home: **C — Agent Action & Effect Semantics**.

### Bounded campaign purpose

Derive and destructively test a technology-neutral Harness criterion for what operational standing may be assigned to an Invocation's external realization from bounded evidence, how unresolved/partial/duplicate/delayed/compensating cases must remain represented, and what reconciliation/redispatch/result-attribution obligations follow — **without making Harness the owner of physical causality or domain semantic success**.

### Explicit non-goals

Campaign 3 will not:

- define universal physical causality;
- treat Runtime receipts as metaphysical effect truth;
- define domain semantic success/failure;
- define normative remedy/compensation correctness;
- build the full Operational Accountability Graph;
- solve Boundary Reconfiguration or Multi-Agent mediation;
- create HaF62.

## 6. Required destructive dimensions for Campaign 3

The Campaign 3 Charter must prebind at least:

- intent admitted but never dispatched;
- dispatch evidence with response loss and unknown external realization;
- terminal receipt/evidence without domain semantic success;
- nonterminal standing later reconciled terminal;
- cancellation request racing with possible realization;
- duplicate/retry pressure after unknown effect;
- partial realization;
- delayed realization after local timeout/abandonment;
- compensating action that does not erase prior effect/provenance;
- contradictory or stale observations/evidence;
- multiple Invocations whose external effects interact or invalidate assumptions;
- physical effect/evidence/result/semantic-success separation.

If partial/delayed/compensating/interfering cases lack current engineering fixtures, they remain conceptual falsifiers rather than being simulated into fake implementation authority.

## 7. Cross-implementation falsification rule

The criterion must be stated without SQLite/Python/current receipt-class identity. Current Tool/Provider/Runtime bridge mechanisms may dogfood it, but the campaign must explicitly retain the limit that one implementation family does not prove universal invariance.
