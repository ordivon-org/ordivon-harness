# Operational Effect Standing & Reconciliation
## Unknown, Partial, Duplicate, Delayed & Compensating External Realization

**Control task:** `task:harness-campaign3-operational-effect-standing-20260818`  
**Programme:** C — Agent Action & Effect Semantics

## 1. Research question

Given a bounded Harness Invocation and externally grounded evidence around its execution, what effect-related operational standing may Harness responsibly carry, what must remain unresolved, and what reconciliation/continuation/redispatch/result-attribution obligations follow — without making Harness the owner of physical causality, external-system truth or domain semantic success?

Campaign 3 does not ask “what really happened in the world?” in the metaphysical sense. It asks what **evidence-relative operational claims and obligations** Harness may safely preserve.

## 2. Prebound ownership firewall

Harness owns:

- Invocation identity/lineage and bounded control relation;
- mediation of externally supplied dispatch/effect/result evidence;
- evidence/provenance/currentness binding;
- effect-related claim standing relative to available evidence;
- reconciliation and redispatch/continuation obligations that follow from those standings.

Harness does **not** own:

- Runtime physical execution truth;
- arbitrary external-system causal truth;
- World/domain semantic success;
- Normative legitimacy of compensation/remedy;
- Network substrate truth.

Therefore every Campaign-3 result must remain restatable as an evidence-relative operational relation.

## 3. Prebound multi-axis relation stack

Campaign 3 rejects one universal `effect_status` as its starting ontology.

### Axis 0 — Effect Evidence Projection Standing

Is the evidence projection itself admissible?

- `VALID_EFFECT_EVIDENCE_PROJECTION`
- `INVALID_EFFECT_EVIDENCE_PROJECTION`
- `EFFECT_EVIDENCE_UNDERDETERMINED`

Invalidity includes forged receipt/provenance, impossible Invocation binding, stale evidence represented as current without support, or evidence that contradicts its claimed source/lineage.

### Axis A — Dispatch Standing

What does authoritative evidence establish about dispatch of the Invocation across the Harness/external-realization boundary?

- `NOT_DISPATCHED_ESTABLISHED`
- `DISPATCHED_ESTABLISHED`
- `DISPATCH_STANDING_UNRESOLVED`

Dispatch standing is not effect standing.

### Axis B — Realization-Claim Standing

What may Harness claim **from available evidence** about external realization, without converting the claim into owner-independent physical truth?

Initial vocabulary:

- `NO_REALIZATION_EVIDENCE`
- `REALIZATION_UNRESOLVED`
- `PARTIAL_REALIZATION_EVIDENCED`
- `REALIZATION_EVIDENCED`
- `REALIZATION_EVIDENCE_CONFLICTED`

`NO_REALIZATION_EVIDENCE` explicitly means absence of admissible realization evidence, not evidence of no realization.

### Axis C — Reconciliation Standing

What must happen before Harness can safely collapse uncertainty for its own downstream operational decisions?

- `NO_RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED_FOR_BOUND_USE`
- `RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED`
- `RECONCILIATION_IN_PROGRESS`
- `RECONCILIATION_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`
- `EXTERNAL_EFFECT_DECISION_REQUIRED`

Reconciliation may advance standing by a new evidence/lineage edge; it must not rewrite prior uncertainty out of history.

### Axis D — Continuation / Redispatch Obligation

What may Harness infer about further operation?

Campaign 3 deliberately avoids a universal `RETRY_SAFE` state. Instead it prebinds obligation classes:

- `NO_PRIOR_DISPATCH_OBLIGATION` — only when non-dispatch is established strongly enough for the bounded use;
- `REDISPATCH_PROHIBITED_PENDING_RECONCILIATION`;
- `REDISPATCH_REQUIRES_EXTERNAL_ADMISSION`;
- `CONTINUATION_REQUIRES_EFFECT_STANDING`;
- `NO_EFFECT_RELATED_BLOCK_FOR_BOUND_USE`.

Whether a repeated operation is semantically/idempotently safe may belong to domain/Runtime/external capability semantics and cannot be invented by Harness from missing evidence.

## 4. Proposition P3 — Evidence-Relative Effect Mediation

A technology-neutral Harness theory can mediate external realization without annexing causality if it preserves separate evidence, dispatch, realization-claim, reconciliation and continuation-obligation relations.

### P3-A — Evidence validity precedes effect claims

Invalid/forged evidence cannot justify stronger realization standing merely because its content appears plausible.

### P3-B — Dispatch != realization

Dispatch evidence establishes boundary crossing/admission to external realization, not physical effect.

### P3-C — Absence of receipt/evidence != absence of effect

Missing terminal evidence after dispatch creates unresolved realization standing, not a no-effect fact and not automatic retry permission.

### P3-D — Terminal evidence != domain semantic success

A terminal execution/observation receipt may settle some Harness/Runtime standing while leaving domain success, completeness or correctness external.

### P3-E — Cancellation request != non-realization

Cancellation/abort intent or acknowledgement does not retroactively establish that no external effect occurred unless external evidence actually supports that claim.

### P3-F — Reconciliation appends standing; it does not erase uncertainty history

A later terminal/reconciled state must preserve the earlier unresolved/nonterminal standing in lineage.

### P3-G — Partial realization is not scalar failure/success

If authoritative evidence establishes only partial external realization, Harness must preserve that partial standing rather than flattening it into success/failure.

### P3-H — Delayed realization can outlive local timeout/abandonment

Local timeout, response loss, caller abandonment or Run pause does not prove external non-realization.

### P3-I — Duplicate/retry hazards are evidence + Invocation-semantics relative

Logical dedupe/fencing may prevent some duplicate dispatches, but Harness cannot infer absence of duplicate external realization solely from shared Invocation labels or retry count.

### P3-J — Compensation != history erasure

Later compensating action may change current external/domain state, but it does not make the prior effect disappear from provenance/attribution history.

### P3-K — Observation/result/semantic-success remain separate

Observation evidence can support realization claims; Result attribution can summarize bounded operational output; semantic success remains owner/domain relative.

### P3-L — Interacting effects may force external decision/reconciliation

When multiple Invocations interact such that local evidence cannot determine the relevant combined standing, Harness must preserve dependency/uncertainty and request external/domain resolution instead of inventing causal composition.

## 5. Rival models

### R1 — Dispatch Equals Effect

Once dispatch is established, physical effect is treated as established.

### R2 — Receipt Equals Physical Truth

A terminal receipt is treated as complete, owner-independent truth about external causal reality.

### R3 — No Receipt Means No Effect / Retry Safe

Missing terminal receipt after dispatch is interpreted as no effect and permission to retry.

### R4 — Cancellation Means No Effect

Cancellation request/acknowledgement is treated as proof that no external realization occurred.

### R5 — Scalar Success/Failure Effect State

All effect-related standing is collapsed into one success/failure/unknown field.

### R6 — Compensation Erases Prior Effect

A compensating action is treated as if the original effect never occurred for provenance/attribution.

### R7 — Invocation Identity Prevents Duplicate Realization

Shared Invocation identity/digest/fence is treated as universal proof that only one physical effect occurred.

### R8 — Observation Equals Semantic Success

An observed external result is treated as proof of task/domain correctness/completion.

### R9 — Reconciliation Rewrites History

A later terminal result replaces prior unresolved/nonterminal evidence rather than linking to it.

### R10 — Local Lifetime Bounds External Effect Lifetime

Run/process/timeout/caller lifetime is treated as the lifetime of external realization.

## 6. Prebound falsifier classes

P3 must be revised/rejected if any fixture shows:

### F1 — Hidden causality annexation

The criterion cannot be stated without Harness deciding physical/world causal truth beyond authoritative external evidence.

### F2 — False retry permission

The criterion permits redispatch under unresolved possible realization without externally grounded idempotency/admission support.

### F3 — False no-effect conclusion

Timeout/cancel/no-receipt is classified as no effect despite a counterexample where delayed/unknown realization remains possible.

### F4 — False terminal collapse

Terminal execution evidence forces semantic success or completeness that the external/domain owner does not establish.

### F5 — Partial-effect flattening

A partial realization case cannot be represented without collapsing to success/failure.

### F6 — Compensation history erasure

The model cannot preserve original-effect provenance once compensation occurs.

### F7 — Duplicate-realization blindness

The model assumes logical dedupe/Invocation identity universally prevents multiple external effects.

### F8 — Contradictory evidence collapse

Conflicting/stale evidence is forced into one current truth without preserving conflict/reconciliation standing.

### F9 — Interaction collapse

The model claims combined effect semantics for interacting Invocations without required external/domain composition authority.

### F10 — Hidden implementation dependence

The surviving relation requires concrete SQLite/Python/current receipt status names rather than operational evidence roles.

Apply P3 and R1–R10 to T1–T18. Explicitly test whether `Effect Standing` is still too truth-like and whether the relation should be renamed to `Realization Evidence Standing` or another evidence-relative form.

### Round 3 — Owner-boundary + Campaign 1/2 compatibility audit

Verify Runtime/external physical truth, domain semantic success, Normative remedy, Network truth and Accountability boundaries. Check composition with Campaign-1 recovery and Campaign-2 Context relations.

### Round 4 — Closeout

Classify:

- `CRITERION_SUPPORTED_IN_SCOPE`
- `CRITERION_REVISED`
- `CRITERION_FALSIFIED`

Foundation pressure remains a separate classification.

## 10. Stop conditions

Stop rather than expand scope when:

- physical causality itself becomes the primary problem;
- arbitrary domain success/correctness is needed to classify standing;
- compensation/remedy legitimacy becomes the primary problem;
- full Accountability Graph becomes necessary;
- interacting effects require domain-specific causal composition unavailable to Harness;
- current engineering lacks an information-positive fixture for a rich-effect case;
- evidence supports only evidence-relative claim standing and not a general Effect ontology.

The last stop condition is a valid positive result, not failure.
