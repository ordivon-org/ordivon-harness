# 10 — FOUNDATION FAMILIES

These A–I families are the accepted consolidation architecture for navigation and explanatory compression. They are **not** new Foundation IDs, do not renumber HaFs, and do not erase secondary axes or cross-owner bridges.

## A — Boundary, Agency & External Mediation

**HaF0, HaF1, HaF2, HaF25, HaF30, HaF51, HaF54, HaF56**

Harness is cut-relative mediation around Agent/Task/World/authority boundaries, not a protected substance. This family covers problem-space boundary relativity, Agent role, Harness relation, specification mediation, identity under change, reference/binding, functional placement, and boundary exposure.

Standing: strong Harness-native core.

## B — Epistemic Ingress, Representation & External State

**HaF3, HaF4, HaF5, HaF9, HaF22, HaF35**

Observation, representation, retention/recall, uncertainty, distinguishability/diagnosis, and externally maintained epistemic state are typed and provenance/scope relative. Representation/evidence never becomes truth merely by being retained.

Standing: strong Harness-native core.

## C — Action, Control, Selection & Prospective Execution

**HaF6, HaF7, HaF8, HaF24, HaF36, HaF50, HaF57, HaF61**

Action/effect, feedback, typed time/order, metacontrol, prospective plan validity, adjustable control allocation, operational selection, and Run-internal control position form a control family without a universal scalar decision/control ontology.

Standing: strong Harness-native core, with Runtime/domain bridges where physical effects or external actions are involved.

## D — Evaluation, Evidence, Explanation & Assurance

**HaF10, HaF23, HaF27, HaF28, HaF29, HaF31, HaF32, HaF33, HaF34**

Evaluative structure, measurement/validation, evidence dependency, reflexive regimes, explanation, contestability, lifecycle/compositional assurance, and responsibility attribution compose a claim-relative assurance family.

Standing: mixed native/bridge. Harness may own evidence/claim/assurance mediation; evaluative, causal, legal, normative, or domain truth remains with the corresponding owner.

## E — Authority, Governance & Normative Lifecycle

**HaF11, HaF12, HaF37, HaF38, HaF39, HaF40, HaF42, HaF43, HaF47, HaF49**

Authority/delegation, institutions, withdrawal, commitments, revocable permission, composite authorization, due process, remedy, policy lineage, and finality remain typed mediated relations/processes.

Standing: bridge-heavy to Ordivon Normative, Host, Human and domain owners. The family must never be presented as universal Harness-owned normative ontology.

## F — Communication, Strategy & Collective Interaction

**HaF13, HaF26, HaF41, HaF44, HaF45, HaF46**

Strategic interaction, pragmatic grounding, dialectic, targeted influence, testimony/reputation, and dynamic collective composition are externally mediated interaction structures.

Standing: bridge-heavy to Human, Normative, Host, Network and domain owners.

## G — Resources, Service & Distributed Coordination

**HaF14, HaF15, HaF16, HaF17, HaF18, HaF48**

Production/capability formation, resource/capacity/dependency, allocation/scheduling, concurrency, distributed/partial-failure semantics, and progress/liveness form a coordination family.

Standing: bridge-heavy. Physical resources, execution, transport and service truth remain with Runtime/Network/domain owners.

## H — Protocol, Security & Information Boundary

**HaF19, HaF20, HaF21**

Protocol/schema/version compatibility, authentication/trust/security evidence, and information-flow/privacy are boundary contracts and evidence structures.

Standing: bridge-heavy. Network/Runtime/Security/domain owners retain substrate and external truth.

## I — Operational Identity & Orchestration

**HaF52, HaF53, HaF55, HaF58, HaF59, HaF60**

Task episode, context frame, execution reproduction contract, result attribution, invocation lineage, and bounded Run episode are the core Harness operational identity family.

Standing: strong Harness-native core, while Host Task and Runtime Job/Attempt identities remain separate owner-native objects.

## Partition invariant

A–I provide one primary home for every exact identity HaF0–HaF61. Primary home does not imply exclusive semantics. Cross-family views and owner bridges remain first-class.
