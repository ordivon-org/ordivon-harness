## 2. Final semantic core — FOR

Federation is not modeled as one enlarged Agent/Run/Context.

The technology-neutral core is **Federated Operational Relations (FOR)** over locally bounded `AgentOperationalSubject S_i` instances.

Each subject retains local:

- Run / continuation identity;
- Effective Decision Context;
- authority/exposure standing;
- Invocation lineage;
- Realization Claim evidence standing;
- OCSS accountability standing.

Federation relates those local structures through typed edges:

1. shared-work / Host relation;
2. delegation contract;
3. delivery / admission / adoption;
4. Context/evidence transfer;
5. shared external claim identity with subject-relative evidence standing;
6. distinct Invocation / duplicate-effect relations;
7. cross-subject OCSS adoption/binding;
8. branch/partition continuation;
9. history-preserving reconciliation;
10. local vs shared/Host completion relations.

No default federation-wide cognition, Run identity, effect state or accountability graph is admitted.

## 3. Host boundary

**PASS.**

Host owns durable Host Task continuity plus Host Journal/CAS/fencing/handoff authority.

One Host Task may relate multiple local Harness Runs/subjects. This does not make those Runs one federation Run.

Likewise, one local CompletionProposal does not complete peers or the Host Task.

`Shared Host Task != Shared Harness Run`  
`Local CompletionProposal != Host Task completion`.

Persistent purposive coordination remains derived/cross-owner rather than exclusively Host-owned.

## 4. Runtime boundary

**PASS.**

Runtime owns Workspace/Job/Attempt/process and physical execution/evidence truth.

Multiple subjects may dispatch distinct Invocations that result in multiple Runtime Jobs/Attempts. Harness federation does not deduplicate them by shared goal or claim identity.

`Agent subject identity != Runtime process/Job identity`.

A Runtime receipt may be adopted by more than one subject, but its issuer-owned claim remains Runtime-grounded.

## 5. Network boundary

**PASS / NOT DIRECTLY DOGFOODED.**

Network owns inter-locus capability/transport/path/service truth.

A message/evidence item being successfully delivered across a Network boundary does not establish remote Agent admission, adoption or cognition.

Therefore:

`Network Delivery != Remote Adoption != Remote Cognition`.

Partition/reachability protocol design remains Network/distributed-system work. Campaign 6 studies only the Harness-side operational relations once owner-grounded delivery/reachability evidence exists.

## 6. Normative boundary

**PASS.**

FOR may preserve a delegation contract's scope/currentness/revocation evidence. It does not determine whether delegation is legitimate, fair or normatively sufficient.

Delegation may attenuate operational authority and is not a copy of all caller authority.

`Delegation Relation != Normative Legitimacy`  
`Delegation != Truth-Owner Transfer`.

Revocation changes future admissible action. It does not erase prior authorized/realized history.

## 7. Human boundary

**PASS.**

`AgentOperationalSubject` is an operational Harness role, not a theory of human personal identity, cognition, team organization or social responsibility.

A human may participate as an external actor/source/authority, but Human owns motivation, cognition, phenomenology and native social organization.

Campaign 6 therefore does not become a theory of human teams or organizations.

## 8. World / domain boundary

**PASS.**

Multiple subjects may refer to the same external/domain Realization Claim `Q` while holding different local evidence standings.

This does not make truth subject-relative. The **truth owner remains external/domain-owned**; only each subject's admissible evidence standing is local.

`Shared Q != Shared Evidence Standing`  
`Subject-relative evidence != subject-relative truth`.

## 9. Computing / Provider boundary

**PASS.**

AgentOperationalSubject identity is not inferred from model/provider instance identity.

One subject may survive provider replacement if local operational identity/OPUR obligations hold; multiple subjects could also share a provider/process without identity collapse.

Model/provider execution truth remains with the corresponding execution/Computing boundary.

## 10. Accountability boundary

**PASS.**

Campaign 4 OCSS remains local-use-contract relative.

A's evidence/support can be visible or cited by B without B thereby acquiring adequate support standing. B must explicitly admit/adopt/bind evidence under B's local OCSS use contract.

`Evidence Visibility != Cross-Subject Accountability Adoption`.

Common-cause dependency remains explicit: two Agents repeating evidence from one source do not create independent corroboration.

## 12. Campaign-2 compatibility — Context

**PASS.**

Each subject maintains a local Effective Decision Context.

Delivery/visibility of an artifact is not sufficient for Context adoption, and identical source bytes do not imply equivalent local Context because authority/currentness/other evidence can differ.

`Shared Artifact != Shared Effective Context`.

## 13. Campaign-3 compatibility — Realization

**PASS.**

A shared Q can have different local evidence standing without contradiction.

Distinct subjects issuing distinct Invocations do not collapse Invocation cardinality or external realization risk.

`Federation Does Not Deduplicate Invocation or Effect Risk`.

Delegation revocation cannot erase prior supported realization provenance.

## 14. Campaign-4 compatibility — OCSS

**PASS.**

Cross-subject support requires explicit adoption/binding. Graph reachability or evidence citation does not globalize support.

Reconciliation may update current local/federated relations while preserving branch/evidence history.

## 15. Campaign-5 compatibility — OPUR

**PASS.**

Moving, splitting, delegating or re-binding an operational cut does not bypass OPUR.

Federation transitions must preserve/readmit required identity, Context, authority, realization and accountability obligations under the relevant use contract.

`Federation Change != Assumed Equivalence`.

## 17. Derived laws admitted from Campaign 6

### Federation is Local Subjects + Typed Relations, not a Global Run

Federation composes locally bounded operational subjects through explicit relations rather than one enlarged Run/state object.

### Shared Work != Shared Run != Shared Context

A common task/goal does not collapse local identity or cognition.

### Operational Subject Identity != Infrastructure Identity

Process/provider/channel/thread identity is neither necessary nor sufficient for AgentOperationalSubject identity.

### Delegation != Authority Copy != Truth-Owner Transfer

Delegation is directional, scoped, attenuable/currentness-sensitive and does not transfer semantic truth ownership.

### Delivery != Admission != Adoption != Cognition

Transport/exposure, local admissibility, operational adoption and cognition are distinct.

### Shared Realization Claim != Shared Evidence Standing

Subjects can refer to one Q while holding different admissible evidence standing.

### Federation Does Not Deduplicate Invocation or Effect Risk

Distinct subject-local Invocations remain distinct and can create duplicate/conflicting realization pressure.

### Cross-Subject Accountability Requires Explicit Evidence Adoption/Binding

Visibility/citation does not transfer OCSS support standing.

### Local Completion != Federated/Host Completion

A local completion proposal cannot close peers/shared work without owner-level completion authority.

### Shared Ancestry != Federation Convergence

Common lineage does not collapse partition/successor branches into one Run or current state.

### Federation Reconciliation is History-Preserving

Later convergence/settlement must preserve prior branch/evidence history.

### Delegation Revocation != Prior Effect Erasure

Revocation changes future authority; it does not delete prior Invocation/effect/provenance standing.

These are project-level derived laws, not Foundations.
